Architectural Concept

A dialogue of raw concrete, exposed brick and corten steel.

Named as a thoughtful portmanteau honoring homeowners Vaishali and Gaurang, Vairaag merges modern-rustic sensibilities with raw concrete, exposed brick, and bespoke Corten steel jalis that cast intricate shadow poetry into peaceful domestic spaces.

  • Laser-cut Corten steel jali screens functioning as acoustic buffers and artistic light modulators
  • Handcrafted exposed brick masonry laid in textured courses alongside raw board-formed concrete
  • Textured Oikos Italian wall plasters harmonized with bespoke brass-inlaid teak joinery
  • Generous semi-covered garden verandahs facilitating cross-ventilation in the western climate

Located along the developing suburban fringe of Bill Road in Vadodara, Vairaag stands as a sanctuary of quiet luxury. The architects conceptualized the dwelling as an acoustic and thermal refuge, intentionally isolating noise from the approaching street while opening wide to private rear garden enclosures.

The signature Corten steel jalis celebrate traditional Indian architectural screening through an uncompromisingly contemporary medium. As the sun journeys across Gujarat's sky, the geometric apertures trace animated tapestries of light and shadow across the stone floors, infusing the home with a living, kinetic serenity.
