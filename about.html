The Studio · Vadodara, India

Quiet Luxury —
Architecture in Its Purest Form.

Founded in Vadodara, Foresight Associates is an architectural and interior practice rooted in honest materiality, contextual sensitivity, and the quiet celebration of natural light.

Practice Ethos

Spaces that age with grace and permanence.

We believe that true luxury does not shout; it breathes. It is felt in the weight of a natural stone threshold, the gentle cadence of filtered daylight, and the acoustic calm of a well-proportioned room.

Operating from Gujarat's cultural capital of Vadodara, our studio bridges the historic spatial wisdom of Indian courtyard architecture with the clean, rigorous detailing of international modernism.

Every commission begins with a blank canvas and an in-depth reading of the site's orientation, wind movements, and tree canopy. We avoid decorative superficiality, allowing genuine materials—exposed concrete, tactile brick, natural stones, and handcrafted wood—to express their own innate dignity.

Leadership

The Design Principals

Foresight Associates is guided by principal designers Shivali Agrawal, Kunal Patel, and Azaz Saleh. Rather than adhering to a singular rigid dogma, their collaborative partnership fosters a dynamic synthesis of structural clarity, interior refinement, and material honesty.

01

Shivali Agrawal

Principal Designer

Shivali leads the interior spatial narratives, material textures, and sensory curation across the firm's residential and commercial projects. Her work focuses on how light and handcrafted surfaces shape human well-being and daily tranquility.

02

Kunal Patel

Principal Designer

Kunal orchestrates architectural concept planning, environmental bioclimatics, and volumetric massing. He ensures each structure establishes a deep organic relationship with its landscape and orientation.

03

Azaz Saleh

Principal Designer

Azaz directs structural detailing, artisanal metalwork integration, site execution precision, and innovative fenestration. His focus on craftsmanship ensures that design concepts are translated with meticulous fidelity on site.

Core Pillars

Four Principles of Considered Making

01 · Material Integrity

We celebrate raw, unembellished materials that improve with time and wear—weathered brick, cast concrete, brass, and native stones.

02 · Daylight Choreography

Sunlight is an essential building material. Through courtyards and bespoke jalis, we harness light as an expressive element of space.

03 · Context & Proportion

Every building responds directly to Gujarat's intense climate, utilizing overhangs, thermal massing, and natural breezes to maintain comfort.

04 · Living Flora

Courtyards and indoor planter cores form the spiritual heart of our homes, fostering continuous communion between humans and nature.

Context & Geography

Rooted in Vadodara.

Known historically as Baroda, Vadodara is celebrated for its deep cultural arts lineage, magnificent banyan trees, and majestic royal architecture.

Our studio draws continuous inspiration from this heritage. We honor the city's artistic tradition not through historical pastiche, but by channeling the spirit of thoughtful craftsmanship into progressive, modern architecture suited for contemporary generations.
