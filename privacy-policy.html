Privacy Policy

How Foresight Associates handles project communications, client privacy, and data security.

1. Principles of Discretion

Foresight Associates ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy and confidentiality of individuals who contact our architectural practice. As an architecture studio based in Vadodara, Gujarat, we adhere to strict ethical standards in managing all communications regarding private residential, commercial, or interior commissions.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information solely when you voluntarily submit it via our website inquiry form or direct communication. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, and optional phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Project Specifications: Location, typology (residential, commercial, interior), approximate built-up area, estimated timeline, and project description.
  • Technical Metadata: Anonymized submission timestamps and masked IP hashes for spam mitigation and rate limiting.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your details are used strictly for:

  • Reviewing site suitability and architectural feasibility by our principal designers.
  • Responding to your project inquiry with relevant studio portfolios or consultation scheduling.
  • Maintaining an internal register of commissions and client project briefs.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website does not use intrusive marketing trackers, behavioral profiling, or advertising cookie networks. We load fonts through privacy-conscious CDNs and operate standard web server access logs for technical uptime, network security, and DDoS protection.

5. Data Storage & Retention

Submitted inquiries are securely logged on our server infrastructure and accessible only to authorized studio principals. We retain project inquiry records only for as long as necessary to facilitate ongoing architectural correspondence or to comply with statutory legal requirements.

6. Your Rights & Studio Inquiries

You have the right to request a copy of the information you submitted, request correction of inaccurate data, or request the deletion of your inquiry record from our records. To exercise these rights, please contact the studio through our official inquiry channels.
