Architectural Concept

An artful nucleus of modern architecture and ethnic heritage.

Documented by The Architect's Diary as an 'artful nucleus of modern and ethnic design body,' Shayonam is a tour-de-force of contemporary Indian residential luxury. Spanning nearly 10,000 square feet across four levels, the residence masterfully orchestrates monumental double-height volumes with intricate woodwork and ethnic motifs.

  • Expansive 4-level spatial arrangement accommodating multi-generational family living
  • Central skylit atrium creating vertical acoustic and visual harmony across all four tiers
  • Artisan CNC-carved wooden jalis, fluted timber claddings, and delicate brass accents
  • Sumptuous private master suites featuring integrated dressing lounges and marble ensuites

The spatial program required a delicate balance: providing grand, inspiring public zones for large family gatherings and festive celebrations, while offering intimate, peaceful private quarters for individual generations.

Foresight Associates achieved this through vertical stratification. The lower levels embrace formal entertaining, culinary arts, and family living, anchored around a dramatic central staircase. The upper levels house tranquil bedroom retreats, private lounges, and sacred spaces. Honest concrete finishes pair with warm teak veneers to create an environment that feels both internationally cosmopolitan and deeply rooted in Indian culture.
