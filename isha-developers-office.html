Architectural Concept

Permanent presence through exposed concrete, brick and courtyard core.

Featured on Volume Zero for challenging the temporary, utilitarian nature of typical property site offices, Isha Developers Office is a permanent architectural statement built to last for an extended period amidst wholesome greenery in Vadodara.

  • Permanent architectural structure departing from generic prefabricated modular containers
  • Central courtyard-style core anchoring cross-ventilation, sightlines, and natural light
  • Overhead cast-iron jali canopy casting delicate shadow tapestries across raw brick walls
  • Sloping terracotta roof forms combined with wide granite plinth steps and Kota stone floors

The project commands a strong road-facing visual presence, welcoming prospective homebuyers and business partners with warmth rather than clinical corporate sterility. Broad granite steps lead visitors up into a transparent glass threshold that directly reveals a central open-to-sky planter core.

The material dialogue pairs raw exposed concrete beams with handcrafted red clay brick masonry. Overhead, a cast-iron jali acts as a solar filter, tempering direct sunlight while keeping interior administrative suites cool and visually connected to outdoor greens.
