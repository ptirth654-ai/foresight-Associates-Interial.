Disciplines & Scope · Vadodara, India

Architectural Practice Areas

We bring an integrated, craft-focused perspective to every stage of space-making—from initial site masterplanning to bespoke interior joinery.

01

Architectural Design

Masterplanning, Context & Tectonics

Our architectural practice approaches every commission from first principles—analyzing daylight angles, microclimatic breezes, spatial proportions, and local material traditions. We design enduring residential, commercial, and institutional structures that age gracefully alongside the natural landscape.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

  • Site topography, solar path analysis, and environmental orientation
  • Architectural massing, schematic volumes, and spatial flow diagrams
  • Comprehensive working drawings, MEP coordination, and municipal approvals
  • Material sourcing, facade engineering, and construction quality review
02

Residential Architecture

Villas & Family Sanctuaries

From suburban retreats like Vairaag and Pramukh Villa to grand four-level residences like Shayonam, our residential work crafts homes that nurture deep family life. We focus on seamless indoor-outdoor transitions, acoustic tranquility, and honest materials such as exposed brick, concrete, and native stone.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

  • Bespoke villa masterplanning and private family residence architecture
  • Multi-generational spatial zoning, private suites, and courtyard connectivity
  • Verandah, terrace, and perimeter biophilic landscape integration
  • Climatic overhangs, perforated jalis, and cross-ventilation design
03

Interior Architecture

Materiality, Light & Craft

We treat interior architecture not as superficial decoration, but as an intimate sculptural continuation of the building itself. We curate natural marbles, warm teakwood, textured Oikos plasters, custom CNC metal screens, and tailored mood lighting to evoke quiet, enduring luxury.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

  • Spatial layout optimization and volumetric transitions
  • Bespoke cabinetry, architectural joinery, and custom furniture design
  • Tactile material schedules, color palettes, and artisan wall finishes
  • Circadian lighting design, ethnic art integration, and soft furnishing curation
04

Commercial & Workplace

Distinctive Brand Environments

As demonstrated in the Isha Developers Office, our commercial architecture transcends generic corporate environments. We craft tactile, permanent workplaces centered around natural green courtyards, exposed masonry, and dignified spaces that leave an indelible impression on clients and teams alike.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

  • Corporate headquarters and developer property site offices
  • Client experience pavilions and executive meeting suites
  • Biophilic courtyard cores and daylight-optimized workspaces
  • Commercial-grade sustainable materials and high-traffic durability
05

Renovation & Consultation

Transforming Legacy Structures

For existing residences and commercial properties seeking renewal, we analyze existing structural bones to introduce generous daylight, improved thermal insulation, modern amenities, and timeless aesthetic coherence while respecting the building's authentic character.

Typical Scope & Deliverables

  • Structural feasibility studies and adaptive spatial planning
  • Facade rejuvenation and envelope thermal optimization
  • Modern mechanical, electrical, and plumbing infrastructure integration
  • Turnkey architectural and interior advisory

Our Methodology

The Design Process

From initial dialogue to final handover, every project follows a disciplined sequence designed to cultivate spatial poetry while safeguarding budgets, engineering precision, and timelines.

STAGE 01

Discovery & Site Reading

In-depth analysis of site topography, solar exposure, wind patterns, local building bylaws, and the client's lifestyle aspirations.

STAGE 02

Conceptual Architecture

Exploration of volumetric massing, sketches, tactile mood boards, courtyard layouts, and client design alignments.

STAGE 03

Design Development

Detailed architectural drafting, 3D visualizations, material specifications, engineering coordination, and approval documentation.

STAGE 04

Execution Review

Active on-site architectural review, material benchmarking, craft detailing, and collaborative oversight through handover.
